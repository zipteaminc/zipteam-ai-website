Security

Six questions
IT asks first

01Nothing installed on devices

Each user gets a dedicated cloud environment. The AI can only reach what’s placed there. Nothing is installed or running on employee laptops. All outbound traffic exits through a single fixed IP.

02Isolated by company and by team

Each customer is fully separated. Other customers’ data is technically unreachable. Teams are separated too, so nothing is visible across departments.

03Data stored in Google Cloud, Tokyo region

Zipteam AI runs on Google Cloud. Data is stored in the Tokyo region.

04Our access is limited to three cases

Monitoring, incident investigation, and support you request. Our terms spell this out.

05Tamper-proof audit logs

Logs can’t be altered after the fact. For audits, we export your company’s records only.

06Covered by Macnica’s ISO 27001

Zipteam AI operations are within the scope of Macnica’s ISO 27001 (ISMS) certification.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where do I start?
Book a 30-minute demo. Tell us about your work and what you want AI to change, and we’ll show you the product live. No prep needed. IT is welcome to join.
How is this different from the AI we already use?
Claude Code in Zipteam AI takes blank-page work (plans, proposals, analysis) all the way to a finished file, using your team’s files and playbooks. You decide what it uses, and you can review and change everything. Finished work stays in the shared workspace for the next job. Many teams keep their current tools for everyday tasks and use Zipteam AI for planning and proposals.
What’s the difference between Claude and Claude Code?
Claude answers in a conversation. Claude Code is Claude that reads and writes files. It delivers plans and analysis as files and saves decisions for the next job. In Zipteam AI, you use Claude Code from a browser. That’s it.
What do I need to get started?
A browser. Nothing to install. Sign in with your Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace account. IT sets up SSO once. Claude works with the same account.
Can I use it without a Claude subscription?
No. Zipteam AI requires a Claude subscription. The Team plan is enough to start.
How do I add documents and data?
Drag and drop files into the file list on the left, or right-click and choose Upload. Claude Code can read anything you add. Text, PDF, Word, and PowerPoint are supported. Text and PDF are read most accurately. To save finished files, use Download in the same menu.
Can my whole team use it?
Yes. Each team has a shared drive. Save a file, and teammates see it right away. Personal drafts go in a private folder. A good approach can be saved as a playbook for the whole team.
How does the trial work?
The trial is free. We’ll agree on the length and number of users after the demo.
What does it cost?
We’ll walk you through pricing on the demo call, based on your team size and how you plan to use it.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your account and data are deleted promptly after the contract ends. Deleted data can’t be recovered, so download anything you need before the end date.
