Notice of Service Discontinuance

Zipteam will close on February 10, 2027

Dear Zipteam users,

Since 2022, Zipteam has helped leaders build better teams by making the skills inside their companies visible. On February 10, 2027, Zipteam will close.

We’re grateful to everyone who built their teams with us. Zipteam started from one belief: breakthroughs only come from great teams. That belief hasn’t changed, and it’s why we’re carrying it forward in a new form.

What happens next

  • Through February 10, 2027 (PST): Zipteam stays open as usual.
  • From February 11, 2027: sign-in will be turned off.
  • On February 17, 2027: all accounts and workspace data will be permanently deleted.

What to do before then

If there’s anything in your workspace you want to keep, please download it by February 10, 2027. Need a hand? Email zipteam-support@macnica.co.jp and we’ll help.

If you’d rather close your workspace sooner, let us know at the same address and we’ll take care of it.

Where Zipteam goes from here

Our mission is still to build dream teams for breakthroughs. We’re now pursuing it with Zipteam AI, a secure, shared workspace where your team works with an AI teammate to turn ideas into finished work. If you’re curious, take a look at www.zipteam.com.
